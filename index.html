Ricchet

Privacy policy, effective November 12, 2026

The short version

Ricochet does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no Ricochet accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, tricks, best scores, and settings) is saved only on your iPhone, so you can pick up where you left off. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Leaderboards (Game Center)

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best scores for Climb, Rush, and Best line are sent to Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see them next to your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Ricochet never sees your Apple ID, email, or contacts. You can sign out of Game Center, or hide from leaderboards, in Settings › Game Center on your iPhone.

Network

Apart from Game Center, the game runs entirely offline and makes no network requests.

Ratings prompt

The game may show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt once. Apple handles that prompt, and Ricochet receives no information from it.

Children

Ricochet is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
